# LAB Plus 0.8.10

This release collects the work from local versions 0.8.1 through 0.8.10 since the previous public 0.8.0 release. It expands the LAB tools, adds the Invisible numbers window and HUD, and corrects interaction countdowns to follow the player release event where that event can be verified.

## Installation

Download `labplus-0.8.10-win-x64.zip`, extract the entire archive and run **LABPlus.exe**. Keep its included folders and DLLs together. This is a Windows x64, framework-dependent build requiring **.NET 10 Desktop Runtime x64** and **Microsoft Edge WebView2 Evergreen Runtime**. `SHA256SUMS` contains the archive's SHA-256 checksum.

Select the intended `DOOMEternalx64vk.exe` and enable its matching outbound firewall block, for example through DESRU. Launching is blocked until that executable has the required rule. LAB Plus inspects firewall state without changing rules. A native DLL update requires a full game restart.

## LAB tab, launch policy and interface

- The dedicated **LAB** tab brings together the original LAB controls, including three independent speedometers, signed vertical speed, Modbot/Q/E input protection, red-screen opacity and AMD driver-line hiding. Console controls use the `labplus_` prefix.
- The performance indicator row retains the requested input-protection and modified red-screen-opacity indicators. Unrelated feature indicators no longer fill that row.
- Lighting Fix is limited to the audited early game versions **1.0, 1.1 and 2.0** on eligible NVIDIA hardware. AMD and unsupported game versions do not receive an active lighting correction.
- The launcher now reports: `Launch blocked: enable firewall rule for the selected DOOM Eternal version(e.g. via DESRU).`
- The executable is named **LABPlus.exe**, with the Plus emblem in the executable resources and interface. The game selector uses the LAB-style icon beside Launch game.
- The title bar has a visible separator; tool-window focus no longer leaves the minimize control highlighted. Trigger Types is a clearly framed button below trigger visibility. Help focuses on controls users need.

## Changed cheat cvars and player modes

- Changed cheat cvars are displayed below the LAB Plus indicators using the DESRU-Enhanced filtering approach. Ordinary menu settings such as volume and difficulty are excluded.
- Active **noclip**, **notarget** and **god** modes are observed from the game's player state, including changes made through argument-free toggle commands.
- The cvar display uses compact text and pages overflowing results so that additional modified cvars remain accessible.

## Collision and research tools

- World collision, triggers and invisible walls have consistent category colors and independent outline/fill appearance, including fill opacity. Trigger class and folder selections persist.
- Collision traversal, caching and render paths have performance fixes; disabled tools avoid unnecessary work. Collision observations do not alter physics.
- The crosshair Volume inspector, Support plane and Active encounters HUD panels stack in that order, closing gaps when earlier panels are disabled.
- Debug HUD text size is adjustable through LAB Plus controls and `labplus_` commands. Long content wraps and uses additional columns within the viewport.
- Research retains manual timelines, markers and CSV export; Movement Lab attempts and route overlays; navigable entity-reference graphs; before/after level comparisons; and research points with physical/camera data.

## Invisible numbers: enemies and glory-kill windows

- Living enemies are grouped by active encounter with current and maximum health. Optional world HP bars follow enemy positions.
- Enemy health/GK text uses the packaged Eternal UI 2 font. HUD placement and rendering account for the viewport and configurable text size.
- Supported stagger/glory-kill observations use the actual engine reaction and deadline fields. Unsupported or unreadable states remain unavailable instead of becoming guessed timers.
- Source-layout and runtime checks distinguish current entities from stale references and preserve the original LAB features independently of the research bridge.

## Invisible numbers: countdowns and speedrun techniques

- Equipment countdowns cover Flame Belch, frag grenades and ice bombs where their engine fields and clock are supported. Inventory ownership prevents uncollected equipment from incorrectly appearing ready.
- Powerup countdowns observe engine status effects, including Quad damage, Berserk and Overdrive. Paused and unavailable durations retain their meaning.
- Sticky Bomb shows available charges, the next individual recovery and the separate empty-magazine reload. Missing charges have an ordered recovery queue on the right. When a full reload restores every charge together, those entries share its one deadline.
- Verified maximum durations appear beside remaining countdowns. Uninitialized or unsupported totals stay unknown; the display does not substitute fixed assumptions or a sampled high-water mark.
- Mantles use actual ledge-grab start/end times. Separate melee, interaction and scripted sync observations avoid copying one unrelated animation's countdown into another row.
- The 1.1 timer clock and powerup-lifetime reader are now audited alongside 1.0 and 5.1, fixing the earlier missing-clock behavior on 1.1.
- BATS and Fast DBlade report observed game state. BATS normal-rate checks cover the audited 1.0/1.1 paths; Fast DBlade follows the prepared Arbalest charge effect and its actual consumption. Unsupported weapon timing stays unavailable.

## 0.8.10 interaction correction

The Praetor Suit Point pickup can return control while its NPC animation continues. A whole current-clip countdown therefore overstates the player's lock.

The corrected observation resolves the **player model's** timeline and the authored **`ae_releasePlayerFromSync`** event, verifies its engine registry identity, and ends the player-lock observation when the player is released. In the demonstrated 1.1 pickup, release is authored at frame 94 of a 30-fps animation: approximately **3.133 seconds**, while the continuing master/NPC clip spans **6.633 seconds**. Event processing follows the game's update timing; no measured stopwatch correction is added.

The NPC's frame-94 `ae_disableClip` and `ae_hideMesh` events are separate events and are not used as substitutes for player release. Unsupported interactions and time mappings retain explicit unavailable/current-clip scope rather than claiming an exact return-of-control countdown. Player-lock timing is enabled for the verified 1.1 route. Earlier supported timer profiles retain their current-clip display. Event-registry lookup code is independently audited for 1.0, 1.1 and 5.1; the positive Praetor demonstration was performed on 1.1.

## Compatibility, checkpoints and validation

The collision reader recognizes 22 audited executable profiles from 1.0 through 6.66 Rev 3.2. Individual research features have narrower support and expose unavailable states when their source cannot be verified. Optional research HUD/console integration requires a compatible already-loaded meathook; it is not bundled. Original LAB adapters remain independent. Native research cvar creation is audited for 6.66 Rev 3.

Local 0.8.10 acceptance passed 21 native fixture suites, 24 isolated Vulkan/GPU cases, launcher/settings/protocol checks, 294 HUD checks and the managed UI build. The Praetor release event was observed in a live 1.1 demonstration and replayed through the production helper in a recorded-data fixture.

The publication workflow builds the native DLLs, executes all 21 native fixture programs plus the launcher/HUD fixtures, publishes the x64 application, checks `LABPlus.exe` FileVersion and validates the 29-file runtime ZIP before creating the release. GUI, Vulkan/GPU and live-game acceptance are separate local checks; a CI fixture pass alone does not establish live behavior on every executable. Feature-level evidence and limitations are recorded in `docs/validation`.
